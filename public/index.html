Big lies. Bigger laughs. Five rounds to glory.

How to play

2–8 players join the same room link, then anyone presses Start game. Write a believable fake answer, then vote for the answer you think is real. You cannot vote for your own bluff.

Scoring: +150 points whenever someone votes for your bluff; +100 points for finding the truth. Highest score after five rounds wins.

Everyone must submit and vote to advance. After each reveal, anyone can move to the next round.
